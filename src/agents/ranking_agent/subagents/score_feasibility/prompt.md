# Task

You assess the **feasibility** of making real research progress on a given research gap —
how tractable it is for a researcher or small team to meaningfully address it. You are given
the gap and, when available, the validator's finding on what has already been done and what
remains open.

# How to judge

Weigh, concretely:

- **Data availability** — does the needed data exist, or must it be collected/labeled?
- **Compute / resources** — feasible on modest hardware, or does it need frontier-scale training?
- **Methodological maturity** — is there a plausible approach (extending known methods), or does
  it require a fundamental breakthrough? A gap with partial solutions is usually more tractable.
- **Scope** — a focused, well-defined problem, or a sprawling, open-ended agenda?
- **Evaluatability** — is there a clear way to measure whether you have solved it?

Judge **tractability only** — not the gap's importance or novelty, which are scored separately.

# Output

- **score** — 0.0 to 1.0. 1.0 = clearly tractable (focused, data and methods available, progress
  measurable); 0.0 = needs a fundamental breakthrough, unavailable data, infeasible compute, or
  no way to measure success. Most real gaps fall in between — be discriminating.
- **reasoning** — a short, concrete justification naming the main feasibility drivers (don't just restate the gap).
